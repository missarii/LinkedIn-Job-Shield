🛡 LinkedIn Job Shield — Settings

Configure how the extension filters job posts on LinkedIn. Changes save automatically to your browser and apply to open LinkedIn tabs.

General

Blocked companies

One company name per line. Job posts from these are hidden immediately.

Blocked keywords / patterns

One phrase per line. Any match adds to the suspicion score.

Whitelist (required criteria)

One term or phrase per line. With REQUIRED on, any job post not containing at least one of these is hidden (as “doesn’t match criteria”). With REQUIRED off, it only gets the ⭐ highlight.

Suspicion scoring weights

Points added when the corresponding signal is detected.

🤖 On-device AI classifier

Runs a free Hugging Face zero-shot model locally in your browser (Transformers.js). No API key, nothing leaves your machine. Model weights + runtime (~100–500 MB) download on first use and are cached.
